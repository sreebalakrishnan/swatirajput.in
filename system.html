Design system

The Spine

The system behind swatirajput.in. Direction: an anatomical plate that has learned to breathe — cool engraved paper, one warm pulse, and a single line running the length of the page. Every token here already exists in index.html. Any new page should pull from this list and add nothing to it.

01 — Why it looks like this

The design has one job: make illegible work legible

Swati’s difficulty is not that people doubt her. It is that nobody can explain what she does, so it gets called magic, which loses her the readers who most need convincing. The page therefore has to hold two registers at once: the precision of the clinical world she trained in, and the warmth of the work she actually does.

That tension produced every decision below. Cool paper and hairline rules and letterspaced keys carry the instrument. Spectral, set large and loose with plenty of rest, carries the hand. The one warm colour is used the way an anatomical plate uses a madder wash — sparingly, to mark what is alive.

02 — Colour

Nine values, and they mean things

The palette is semantic. Held is what has stopped travelling; pulse is what is going through. That mapping comes from the work itself, so it must not be reversed for visual variety.

  • Never

    Held as a text colour. It fails contrast against paper at every size — it exists for 1px rules and 9px dots.

  • Never

    Pulse as a background for long text, or as a decorative fill. One solid button per screen, at most.

  • Never

    A gradient, a drop shadow, or a second accent. If something needs emphasis, use scale, space, or italic.

  • Always

    Night used exactly once per page, for the passage that earns intimacy. On the homepage that is the five accounts.

03 — Type

Two families: one speaks, one instructs

Spectral carries anything in Swati’s voice or about a person — headlines, ledes, story titles, the turn lines. It was designed for screen reading and has an engraved crispness that suits the plate. Weights 300, 400, 500 and italic. Instrument Sans carries anything that instructs — body copy, labels, rows, buttons. Weights 400 and 500 only.

There is deliberately no monospace. A mono label is the current shorthand for “technical” and it reads as a template tell; letterspaced small caps do the same job and belong to the anatomical-plate reference.

  • Measure

    Prose never exceeds 34rem. This is a reading site, and an anxious parent reads it at night.

  • Italic

    Spectral italic marks the body speaking — what is this holding trying to say? Not used for general emphasis.

  • Never

    Centred paragraphs, all-caps headlines, or letterspacing on anything larger than a label.

Section spacing is the only place a viewport unit is allowed, so the page breathes more on a large screen without the type scale drifting.

05 — Layout

Two columns, and the left one is the signature

Above 1024px, every section is a grid of a 9.5rem marginalia column and the prose. The marginalia column holds the section label and a node, and a single hairline runs down its left edge from the hero diagram to the last section. Nodes fill grey as you pass them and madder for the section you are in.

This is the one bold element, and it is content-true rather than decorative: Swati’s whole diagnostic question is whether movement travels from the ankle to the knee to the hip. Reading the page performs the same thing. Below 1024px the spine and nodes are dropped entirely rather than shrunk, and labels sit above their headings.

  • Never

    Add a second sticky element, a fixed header, or a scroll-progress bar. The spine already does that job.

  • New pages

    Reuse .band / .gutter / .content and wrap the sections in .spine. The line and nodes come for free.

06 — Components

Six, and no more than six

Buttons

One solid button per page. Everything else is a bordered secondary or a plain link.

Rows

Hairline-separated rows with an 11rem term column. Used for who comes, before you come, and contact details. Replaces cards — there are no cards in this system.

Story block, on night

An arm that was guarding

A woman whose hand habitually held itself in a protective position. Some weeks later, what she reported was not about her arm. She had been able to set a boundary with her parents for the first time.

Italic Spectral title, body in night text, and the turn of the story lifted to full paper white. One lift per story.

The mark

The favicon is the spine in miniature: an ink stem, the madder pulse arriving from below, and one node where it has reached. Inlined in the head as an SVG data URI, with 32px and 180px PNGs beside it for older browsers and iOS home screens. Held is deliberately not used — at 16px it disappears against paper, and the stem has to hold or the mark reads as a lone dot. This page carries the same mark in ink only, so the two tabs stay apart.

Placeholders

Unwritten copy is marked in madder with a dotted underline (.fill). Search the file for class="fill" to find every outstanding decision. Delete the span, not just the text.

07 — Motion

Two sequences, then stillness

The hero plate draws itself, a madder pulse travels up from the ankle, stalls between the knee and the hip, the gap closes, and it goes through to the head. Roughly seven seconds, once, on load.

The portrait is the second and last: three washes bloom from separate points and run together over two seconds, so that a painting arrives by being painted. It fires when the figure reaches the viewport, not on load — the portrait sits most of the way down the page, so a load animation would play to nobody. It runs once and does not repeat on the way back up.

Beyond those two, the only movement is the spine filling as you scroll and colour transitions on hover. A third sequence would cost the plate its authority; if something new needs attention, give it space instead.

Everything is inside @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference). With reduced motion the plate renders complete, the caption is visible from the start, and the portrait is simply there — nothing is lost, only the performance. The wash also depends on script: the mask is only applied once JavaScript arms it, so without JavaScript, or without IntersectionObserver, the portrait never hides in the first place.

08 — Non-negotiables

The quality floor

  • Contrast

    All text meets WCAG AA. Held is barred from text precisely because it does not.

  • Keyboard

    A skip link, and a visible madder focus ring with 4px offset on everything interactive.

  • Screen readers

    The plate carries a full text description. Section labels are real headings or paragraphs, never image text.

  • Print

    A print stylesheet exists because parents will print this and hand it to a grandparent or a paediatrician. Ink on white, the diagram and navigation dropped, sections kept off page breaks.

  • Weight

    One HTML file, two webfonts, about thirty lines of JavaScript. It should open fast on a phone on Indian mobile data.

09 — If you hand this to a developer

What they need to know

Tokens live in :root in index.html, commented and grouped. There is no build step, no framework, no dependency beyond Google Fonts. To add a page, copy the head, the token block, and the .band markup pattern, then write content. If a new colour or a third typeface feels necessary, the answer is almost certainly a different use of space instead.
